Who We Are

Built to Level
the Playing Field

Matern Law Group was founded on a single conviction: vulnerable members of the public deserve top legal talent in their corner — not just the rich and powerful. From our California offices, our 24 attorneys collaborate on every case, investing in our clients' welfare, not just their case contingencies.

We operate on the front lines of California's most important legal battles — shaping policy, improving conditions in boardrooms, and fighting for those who need it most. Because the law isn't always clear-cut, we bring empathy, transparency, and excellence to every stage of the process.
